Accountable conservation for Somaliland’s wildlife and communities

The problem

Somaliland is a transit point for the illegal trade in live cheetah cubs and other wildlife bound for the Arabian Peninsula. Rangelands are degrading under drought, charcoal production and grazing pressure. Conservation funding has been short-term and hard to verify.

The Fund

Masul Conservation Fund is the conservation and donor-funded pillar of Masul Group, Hargeisa. It supports wildlife protection, community conservation and conservation data. Every activity is recorded in systems partners can audit.

Programmes

  1. Wildlife protection & anti-trafficking: support to the Somaliland Wildlife and Forest Crime Unit and member agencies.
  2. Confiscated wildlife tracking: records from seizure through care, placement and case outcome.
  3. Community conservation: FPIC consultation, conservancy governance and livelihoods.
  4. Youth & conservation education: schools, universities and community leaders.
  5. Conservation data & reporting: surveys, M&E frameworks and partner dashboards.

Why us

The founder, Ibrahim Abdirahman Ismail, spent 2022–2025 as IT & Project Coordinator at CCF Somaliland under the DEFRA-funded LICIT II programme. During that time he:

  • coordinated the launch of the Somaliland Wildlife and Forest Crime Unit (5 ministers, 8 agencies)
  • deployed the national wildlife crime database across enforcement agencies and courts
  • led FPIC consultations in 27+ villages in Awdal
  • helped establish the C1 Awdal community conservancy
  • founded the SACSO network of 30 NGOs

How we report

We agree a results framework with each partner, report quarterly on activities and spending, give dashboard access, and provide field evidence on request.

Partnership

We are seeking programme grants, delivery partnerships and restricted gifts. The first programme is planned for Awdal and will start once funding and agreements are in place.
