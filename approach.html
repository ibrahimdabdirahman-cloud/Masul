Our approach

Every shilling, accounted for.

Conservation funding in fragile settings only works if funders can see what their money did. We built the Fund around that requirement.

Principles we will not trade away.

These apply to every programme, partner and report.

01

Consent before intervention

No community-level work begins without free, prior and informed consent. Consultation continues for the whole programme.

02

Government partnership, not substitution

We strengthen the ministries, agencies and courts responsible for wildlife protection and do not build parallel structures.

03

Evidence behind every figure

Reported results link back to field records, photographs, GPS points and documents that partners can audit.

04

Local delivery, international standards

Programmes are designed and led in Somaliland and managed to PRINCE2 discipline and donor compliance requirements.

05

Restricted funds stay restricted

Funding is tracked against the programme and budget line it was given for.

06

Honest reporting

We report delays, setbacks and lessons learned alongside results.

Safeguards

Governance and safeguarding.

We are putting in place the policies that institutional funders expect before our first programme starts.

  • Safeguarding policy covering staff, volunteers, communities and partners
  • Anti-bribery, anti-corruption and conflict-of-interest policies
  • Financial controls with segregated approval for programme spending
  • Data protection for informant, community and case data
  • Risk register and field security procedures
  • Environmental and animal welfare standards
