Partner with us

Build accountable conservation in Somaliland.

We work with funders, government, conservation organisations and the Somali diaspora. We report to every partner with the same rigour.

Ways to partner.

We agree the scope, indicators and reporting schedule with each partner before any funds move.

Institutional funders

Programme grants

Fund a full programme or a defined component, with a results framework agreed with you.

Conservation organisations

Delivery partnerships

Deliver jointly, using our local presence, government relationships and data systems.

Government & agencies

Technical support

Coordination, training, databases and proposal development for public institutions.

Diaspora & private donors

Restricted gifts

Support a named programme, such as a conservancy, a school cohort or community rangers.

What partners receive

Reporting you can rely on.

  • A results framework agreed at the outset, with baselines and targets.
  • Quarterly progress reports covering activities, spending and variance.
  • Live dashboard access for programmes that include field monitoring.
  • An annual narrative and financial report in your required format.
  • Field evidence on request, including survey records, photographs and documentation, with community consent.
