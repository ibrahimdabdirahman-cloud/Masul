About the Fund

Masul means responsible.

Masul Conservation Fund is the conservation and donor-funded unit of Masul Group. It was set up to give wildlife and communities in Somaliland lasting, accountable protection.

Mission

Protect wildlife. Support communities. Account for everything.

We protect wildlife, support the communities who live alongside it, improve conservation data, and run donor-ready conservation programmes in Somaliland and the Horn of Africa.

The Fund is one of the five core pillars of Masul Group, a multi-unit holding company headquartered in Hargeisa. It links field experience, data systems, donor reporting and community trust. It also shares tools with the other Masul units: Survey Desk for field data, WorkWire for coordination and Masul AI for analysis and reporting.

مسؤل (Masul) means responsible. We chose the name as a working principle for how the Fund operates.

What we stand for.

The five Masul Group values, applied to conservation.

Responsibility

We answer for what we build.

We keep commitments to communities, government and funders without being asked, and report what did not work as well as what did.

Trust

Earned slowly, protected carefully.

Consent comes before any intervention, and consultation continues for the life of a programme.

Protection

Wildlife and people, together.

Species, habitats and the communities who depend on them are protected together.

Legacy

Built for decades.

We build institutions, databases and conservancies that keep working after a grant cycle ends.

Growth

From one rooted core.

We start in Awdal and grow region by region across Somaliland and the Horn of Africa, keeping the same standards.

Our voice

Calm, precise, evidence-first.

We say what we do, then show the evidence. We avoid hype.

Founder

Ibrahim Abdirahman Ismail

IT & Project Coordinator · Wildlife Conservation Specialist · Network Engineer · Founder & CEO, Masul Group.

From 2022 to 2025 Ibrahim served as IT & Project Coordinator at CCF Somaliland under the DEFRA-funded LICIT II programme. He worked on field coordination, database development, government liaison and programme administration, alongside actors including TRAFFIC, IUCN SOS, the U.S. Fish & Wildlife Service and EU partners.

He founded Masul Group in 2025 to carry that institutional and technical knowledge forward. The Conservation Fund is the part of the group closest to that work.

    CCF Somaliland · SACSO founded

    Represented CCF Somaliland at the Global Cheetah Summit in Addis Ababa. Was IUCN-nominated and fully funded to attend the IUCN Africa Conservation Forum in Nairobi. Led the Borama Governors Meeting (100+ participants), which advanced the Awdal conservancy framework.

  4. 2025

    C1 Conservancy · Masul Group founded

    Formally established the C1 Awdal community conservancy at a governance workshop with 113 participants. Founded Masul Group and the Conservation Fund.

Part of Masul Group

One group. Many services.

Masul Group is a holding company based in Hargeisa. Its units cover workflow, mobility, field data, artificial intelligence, conservation, digital services and contracting.

The group’s commercial units help fund its platforms, and the Conservation Fund runs alongside them as the group’s social-impact and donor-funded pillar. The Fund does not have to build its own data or reporting systems because it uses the group’s shared infrastructure.
